# Cocoon Blanket reference notes

The reference uses a restrained commerce/editorial shell with a very slim black utility bar, mostly white/soft-gray pages, tiny dark navigation, oversized thin serif hero typography anchored low in the viewport, and two compact CTA buttons. The visible hero is intentionally sparse: large empty field, one dominant statement, tiny supporting line, and restrained controls.

Down-page content is organized into curated editorial collection moments rather than dense product grids. Large images alternate with short serif headlines, small explanatory copy, and quiet text links. The interaction language is calm, tactile, spacious, and product-led rather than dashboard-like. For KisanSetu, adapt this with farmer imagery, direct-market copy, and a minimal story progression while retaining the same restraint and whitespace.
