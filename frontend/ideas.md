# KisanSetu design direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Field Ledger
Very Brief Intro: A warm, editorial operations interface that treats every transaction like a visible line in a trusted agricultural ledger. It balances earthy material cues with crisp data density.
Probability: 0.06

### Theme Name: Monsoon Signal
Very Brief Intro: A bold, high-contrast product system inspired by monsoon radar, route maps, and agrarian signal flags. It makes live operational intelligence feel urgent but grounded.
Probability: 0.03

### Theme Name: Harvest Commons
Very Brief Intro: A calm, open marketplace language with sun-washed paper tones, cooperative green, and generous typography. It makes the platform feel civic, welcoming, and dependable.
Probability: 0.08

## Selected approach: Field Ledger

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorialism fused with Swiss information design and the tactile logic of a farm ledger: clear hierarchy, visible provenance, and purposeful asymmetry.

### Core Principles
1. Make intelligence legible at the moment of decision, not behind a separate AI surface.
2. Use asymmetry and strong editorial rhythm to make the workflow feel active and human.
3. Pair warm, tactile surfaces with precise operational data so trust feels both emotional and measurable.
4. Treat every recommendation as explainable evidence with a visible reason.

### Color Philosophy
KisanSetu is anchored in a deep field green that signals stewardship and continuity, then balanced with warm grain, clay, and turmeric accents that connect the interface to harvest, soil, and price movement. The palette should feel agricultural without becoming rustic or decorative; chart colors must remain distinct and accessible, with red reserved for risk and amber for attention.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored dashboard shell with a persistent navigation rail and a wide, asymmetric work canvas. Key decisions appear in stacked cards with a deliberate editorial offset: the primary workflow is dense and actionable while the supporting rationale sits in calmer side panels. Avoid centering everything; use rail, split-pane, and horizontal scan patterns.

### Signature Elements
- A narrow “signal rail” motif: small vertical bars and ticks that echo field rows and market charts.
- Ledger dividers: hairline rules, compact metadata, and highlighted cells that make provenance visible.
- A live route ribbon that turns logistics into a visual thread from farm to buyer.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like moving through a well-organized operations room. Hover states reveal context, selected states are pinned with clear contrast, and key actions confirm instantly without theatrical motion. Buttons should feel decisive; recommendation panels should expand to show reasons rather than launch a separate conversational surface.

### Animation
Use short, physical transitions under 240ms: cards lift 2px on hover, tabs slide a 2px underline, and route segments draw in with a restrained 180ms ease-out. Stagger dashboard cards by 40ms on first load, but keep recurring data updates calm with opacity and color changes only. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use `DM Serif Display` for high-level section moments and `Manrope` for all operational UI, metrics, controls, and metadata. Headlines should be compact and editorial, with high contrast between large display lines and small uppercase labels. Metrics use tabular numerals where available; body text stays at 14–16px with generous line-height.

### Brand Essence
KisanSetu is the intelligent bridge between the people who grow and the people who need, built for farmers, FPOs, buyers, and operations teams who want fewer blind spots and fairer movement of produce. Personality: grounded, exacting, connective.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are clear and purposeful; CTAs are direct verbs; microcopy explains what the system decided and why. Avoid generic AI language and avoid inflated promises.

Example lines:
- “Move produce with fewer blind spots.”
- “Recommended because demand is rising in your delivery radius.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact, textless mark built from two interlocking field rows forming a bridge: the upper row bends downward into a small grain motif while the lower row rises to meet it. The mark should read as connection first and agriculture second, with no generic leaf icon.

### Signature Brand Color
Field Green `#235C45` — a deep, ownable green that carries trust, continuity, and the visual weight of a well-kept ledger.

## Style Decisions
- The core product surface is light and warm, not dark neon.
- Intelligence is embedded in listings, matches, allocation, and routes; there is no chatbot or assistant bubble.
- Every recommendation gets a visible “why” affordance.
- Use generated brand artwork only in the prominent shell moments; keep dense data views quiet.
