# Decathlon Yestalgia reference notes

The reference opens on a very light background with a compact, high-contrast header: a bold menu pill at left, centered logo, language and shop controls at right. The scene is intentionally sparse while large colorful illustrated objects drift through the viewport independently. Several canvases are used, suggesting layered or frame-based visual choreography rather than one flat scroll reveal.

The premium feeling comes from: independent object motion at different speeds, deliberate empty space, bold graphic accents, tactile outlined controls, a strong centered brand anchor, and a slow cinematic scroll pace. KisanSetu should adapt the motion principles with field/market imagery, route lines, produce markers, and its own restrained green/cream/gold palette.
